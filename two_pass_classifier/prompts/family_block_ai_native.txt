============================================================
FIXED FAMILY: AI-NATIVE (PriorBinaryVerdict = 1)
============================================================

Choose exactly one subclass from 1A through 1G, then assign RAD-H, RAD-M, or
RAD-L. The family is fixed. Never output a 0-series subclass or RAD-NA.

1A | Foundation Layer
Companies building the base of the AI stack: frontier or foundation models,
novel model architectures, core AI research, ML compute orchestration, model
serving infrastructure, or vector databases. The model or infrastructure is
the artifact other AI products build on.

Key signal: the primary customer is another AI builder, and the company creates
the model or foundational infrastructure rather than an end-user application.
Examples include Anthropic, Mistral, Cohere, Pinecone, Modal, and Together AI.

1B | AI-Native Infrastructure and Tooling
Companies whose primary product serves the AI development lifecycle. This
includes evaluation, fine-tuning, AI observability, prompt management,
synthetic data, AI safety, model monitoring, and LLMOps.

Key signal: the primary customer is an AI developer or AI team. A conventional
database, integration platform, security product, or developer tool with a
recent AI feature is not 1B unless serving AI builders is now the primary
purpose of the product.

1C | Thin LLM Wrapper
Products whose mechanism is a third-party model API with little proprietary
depth. Common signals are a small surface area, commodity prompting, no unique
data flywheel, no material fine-tuning, shallow workflows, and easy replication
by a small team.

The business problem may predate generative AI. The company is still
AI-native because removing the model call breaks this specific product.

1D | Thick LLM Integrator
Products built on third-party models with meaningful proprietary value such as
domain adaptation, unique datasets, deep workflow integration, retrieval or
index infrastructure, enterprise controls, distribution, network effects, or
a strong user-experience moat.

Key distinction from 1C: replacing or recreating the product requires
substantial proprietary work beyond reproducing prompts and API calls.
Key distinction from 1E: core intelligence still primarily flows from
third-party foundation models rather than a deeply proprietary vertical model
or classical ML stack.

1E | Applied Vertical AI
AI purpose-built for a specific domain where AI produces or fundamentally
transforms the domain workflow. Signals include domain-specific datasets,
expert validation, proprietary models, fine-tuning, classical ML pipelines,
and deep integration into a regulated or specialized workflow.

Key distinction from a conventional vertical SaaS product: removing AI
destroys the core product mechanism rather than removing one feature.
Key distinction from 1D: the evidence supports deep domain-specific AI
capability, not only a general model wrapped in a vertical interface.

1F | Autonomous Agent Systems
The core product plans and executes multi-step tasks with tools, memory, or
background operation. It acts in software or the world with limited
step-by-step human approval.

Require concrete action signals such as tool use, browsing, code execution,
scheduled work, background jobs, or multi-step workflow completion. Marketing
use of "agent" is insufficient. A responsive chatbot belongs in 1C or 1D
unless autonomous action is the central mechanism.

1G | Generative Content Platforms
The customer deliverable is generated media such as images, audio, video,
music, or 3D assets. Generation is the product, not one feature inside a
traditional creative suite.

Subclass follows what the product produces. A thin product calling a
third-party media-generation API can still be 1G; its lack of proprietary
depth affects RAD rather than subclass.

------------------------------------------------------------
SUBCLASS TIE-BREAKERS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Foundation models or foundational infrastructure sold to AI builders: 1A.
- Tools for building, evaluating, operating, or securing AI systems: 1B.
- General LLM API product with little proprietary depth: 1C.
- General LLM API product with substantial proprietary depth: 1D.
- Deep domain-specific AI or classical ML system: 1E.
- AI that autonomously plans and acts across steps: 1F.
- Generated media is the customer deliverable: 1G.
- A company training its own models for one vertical domain is usually 1E,
  not 1A.
- A pre-2023 computer vision, NLP, recommendation, or forecasting company can
  be 1E when AI is the core mechanism.
- If evidence cannot distinguish the mechanism within this fixed family, use
  1C as the conservative fallback and set subclass_confidence to 1.

============================================================
RAD: RESOURCE-ADJUSTED AI DEPENDENCY
============================================================

RAD measures structural dependence on current third-party generative AI
infrastructure, adjusted for the company's demonstrated proprietary capability
and resources.

Core counterfactual: if the company had substantial funding and five years,
could it build a competitive product without depending on current third-party
foundation-model APIs?

RAD-H | High Structural Dependency
The product remains structurally tied to third-party frontier capabilities or
model APIs that the company could not credibly reproduce. Signals include
explicit reliance on external models, little proprietary model or data depth,
and a commodity wrapper surface.

RAD-M | Medium or Transitional Dependency
The product depends on external models today but has credible partial
independence through proprietary data, fine-tuning, model development, deep
workflow assets, pre-2023 classical ML, meaningful technical scale, or the
ability to switch providers without losing the whole product.

RAD-L | Low Structural Dependency
The company demonstrably builds and trains proprietary AI at the core, or has
deeply independent AI capability that would survive the loss of third-party
model APIs. Strong evidence is required.

RAD rules:
- 1C is RAD-H or RAD-M, never RAD-L.
- 1D is RAD-M or RAD-L, never RAD-H.
- 1A, 1B, 1E, 1F, and 1G can receive any RAD level based on evidence.
- Before defaulting to RAD-H, look for own-model claims, training,
  fine-tuning, research teams, proprietary datasets, data flywheels,
  pre-2023 ML capability, multi-provider architecture, and credible scale.
- GENAI-ERA companies with no proprietary-depth signal generally default to
  RAD-H.
- PRE-GENAI AI products with credible classical ML or proprietary depth
  generally support RAD-M or RAD-L.
- Funding or headcount alone never proves independence.
- If evidence is insufficient in the fixed AI-native family, use RAD-H and
  set rad_confidence to 1.
