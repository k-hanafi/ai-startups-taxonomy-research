============================================================
FIXED FAMILY: NOT AI-NATIVE (PriorBinaryVerdict = 0)
============================================================

Choose exactly one subclass from 0A through 0C. The family is fixed. Never
output a 1-series subclass.

RAD does not apply to this family. Explain why RAD-NA follows in
rad_reasoning. Do not output rad_score, rad_confidence, or rad_critique.
Assembly assigns RAD-NA and leaves the two non-applicable export fields blank.

------------------------------------------------------------
SORTING PROCEDURE
------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Does the company sell a software product?

Physical goods, retail, manufacturing, or a human-delivered services business
with no software product of its own belongs in 0C. A website, online booking,
or internal use of common software does not make the company a software
business.

Step 2: If the company sells software, has it shipped a genuine and visible AI
feature that meaningfully augments the existing software value proposition or
workflow?

If yes, choose 0B. This captures traditional software/SaaS companies
transitioning into AI-augmented products by adding assistants, copilots,
recommendations, automation, or agent features. Marketing slogans alone do not
count. The AI feature must be real and shipped.

Step 3: Otherwise choose 0A for a software product with no meaningful shipped
AI feature.

Because the family is fixed, unusual AI-core evidence must still receive the
closest 0-series subclass. Do not use 0B when AI is the product mechanism
itself. For a software product in that situation, use 0A and explain the
evidence limitation in subclass_critique.

0A | Traditional Tech or SaaS
The company sells software whose core value does not depend on AI. Examples
include payments infrastructure, logistics platforms, CRMs, ERPs,
marketplaces, conventional developer tools, and no-code platforms.

Key signal: removing every AI reference leaves the product mechanism and main
value proposition materially unchanged. Marketing language alone stays in 0A.

0B | AI-Augmented
The company sells a conventional software product with a genuine, visible AI
feature that improves it. The non-AI core predates or survives removal of AI,
and customers still have the main product without the feature.

Key signal: identify both the durable non-AI product and the shipped AI layer.
An assistant, copilot, recommendation feature, or automation can support 0B
when it augments that durable core.

0C | Non-Tech
The company does not sell software. It sells physical products, retail,
manufacturing, distribution, or human-delivered services such as staffing,
consulting, wealth management, lending, clinics, education, or agency work.

Key signal: software may support operations, but it is not the customer
product.

Tie-breakers:
- Software with no meaningful shipped AI feature: 0A.
- Conventional software with a real AI feature layered onto a surviving core:
  0B.
- Services or physical goods without a software product: 0C.
- Purely manual data labeling is 0A only when sold as a software platform;
  human-delivered labeling services with no software product are 0C.
- AI-assisted annotation can be 0B when a real software platform remains the
  non-AI core.
- "AI-powered" language without product evidence is 0A for software or 0C for
  non-software.
- If evidence cannot identify what the company sells, use 0A with
  subclass_confidence 1.

============================================================
NOT AI-NATIVE OUTPUT CONTRACT
============================================================

Return one JSON object with exactly these fields in this order:

1. subclass
   One of 0A, 0B, 0C.
2. subclass_confidence
   Integer 1 through 5.
3. subclass_reasoning
   Maximum 100 words.
4. rad_reasoning
   Maximum 100 words explaining that RAD is RAD-NA because the fixed family is
   not AI-native.
5. sources_used
   Structured source array defined by the main prompt.
6. subclass_critique
   Maximum 100 words.
